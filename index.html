Services

Six ways to reset a home.

The list Bruna publishes herself. Her team brings all the supplies.

  1. 01

    Residential cleaning

    Weekly, every two weeks or monthly. The same care on every visit, never rushed, never superficial.

  2. 02

    Deep cleaning

    Baseboards, inside the fridge and oven, interior windows. The full reset, room by room.

  3. 03

    Airbnb turnover

    Between checkout and check-in: beds made, towels out, the place set up for the next guest.

  4. 04

    Move in / out cleaning

    Empty rooms, cabinets inside and out, so the keys change hands with the place spotless.

  5. 05

    Post construction

    The fine dust after a renovation, cleared from floors, sills and fixtures.

  6. 06

    Home organizing

    Closets, pantries and the corners where things pile up, put back in order.

Questions

Before you text.

Do you bring the cleaning supplies?

Yes. Bruna’s team brings all supplies.

What extras can I add?

Inside the oven and fridge, interior windows, detailed baseboards, inside cabinets and laundry. Mention them in your text and they go into the quote.

How much does a cleaning cost?

It depends on the type of cleaning, the size of the home and the extras. Send those three things by text and Bruna replies with a quote.

Do you clean homes with pets?

Yes, homes with pets are on the list. Tell Bruna about them when you text.

Do you take care of Airbnb and short-term rentals?

Yes. Turnovers are a big part of the work, and hosts mention it in their reviews. There is a page just for hosts.

How can I pay?

Apple Pay, cash, check, Stripe, Venmo or Zelle, as listed on Bruna’s Thumbtack profile.

Is the team background checked?

Bruna’s background check is verified on her Thumbtack profile.
